Privacy Policy

Tide & Torque Publisher. Effective 9 November 2026.

In short: Tide & Torque Publisher is the Tide & Torque channel's own publishing tool. It connects only to the channel's own YouTube account, uploads only the channel's own videos, and does not collect data from viewers or from anyone else. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Who this applies to

This policy covers Tide & Torque Publisher (the "app"), the automation that publishes videos for the Tide & Torque educational channel. The app is not offered to the public. The only Google account it is authorised for is the account that manages the Tide & Torque YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload and manage videos on the channel. By using the app, the channel operator agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What Google data the app accesses

With the channel operator's permission (OAuth scopes youtube.upload and youtube), the app:

  • uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags;
  • reads the channel's own identity and status (channel id and title, upload status, privacy status, view and video counts) to confirm uploads and avoid duplicates;
  • reads the channel's own uploads list to check whether a video is already published.

The app does not read viewers' data, comments from other people, subscriber identities, messages, or any other account's data.

How the data is used

Only to publish and verify the channel's own videos: uploading them, setting their metadata and publish time, and checking that each upload arrived intact and private or scheduled as intended. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

What is stored, and where

  • The OAuth refresh token that lets the app act for the channel, held as an encrypted secret in the channel's private GitHub repository and used only by its automated workflows. It is never written to logs or files.
  • A production ledger in the same private repository: video ids, scheduled times, upload status and daily API quota counts for the channel's own videos.

No cookies are set and no device information is collected by this website or by the app.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. The channel's own finished videos are also posted to its TikTok and Instagram accounts through Buffer, using a temporary copy hosted on Cloudinary that is deleted once those posts are sent. That process uses the channel's own videos only, not data obtained from Google.

Limited Use

Tide & Torque Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

The refresh token is kept until access is revoked or replaced. Ledger entries are kept while the channel operates and can be deleted at any time by the channel operator.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Once revoked, the app can no longer reach the channel; the stored token stops working and is removed from the repository.

Security

Credentials are kept only in encrypted secret storage, every log line is scrubbed of secret values, and the app requests only the access listed above.

Children

The channel is educational and not directed at children, and the app collects no data from viewers of any age.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the project's public issue page.
