Tide & Torque

Calm, diagram-led explainers about how boats work.

Tide & Torque is an educational channel. Each video explains one mechanism of boating and the physics of the water: why hulls float, what the tide does and does not change, how keels, rudders, propellers and sails work. Every frame is a diagram drawn in code; there is no stock footage.

Watch

Videos are published on the Tide & Torque YouTube channel. Short versions also appear on TikTok and Instagram.

About the publishing tool

Tide & Torque Publisher is the channel's own automation. It uploads the channel's videos to the channel's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services). It is not offered to the public, and it does not ask anyone else to sign in or share data.

Read how it handles data in the privacy policy, and the terms of use.

How the videos are made

Scripts are drafted with AI assistance and checked against public sources such as Wikipedia and NOAA, which each video names in its description. Narration is AI-generated (synthetic voice), and every video is labelled as containing synthetic media.

For education only, not navigation or safety training. Follow official guidance and get proper instruction.
